THE PROJECT

Developed during SolveX 3.0, this collaborative concept combines a simulated IoT bracelet, accident alerts, location sharing, hospital response flows and emergency medical records.

01

The challenge

Reduce avoidable delays between detecting a road accident and coordinating medical assistance.

02

The approach

Combined simulated sensor data and SOS workflows with location-aware hospital coordination, offline considerations and the LDX emergency patient record concept.

03

The outcome

A hackathon prototype and architecture exploring connected emergency response in the Algerian context.
